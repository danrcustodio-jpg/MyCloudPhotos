Organize

        

        

          
By date

          

            Consolidate your library into dated folders (e.g. 2024/2024-07).
            Photos leave legacy folders like Google Photos, drive root, or custom albums.
            Uses camera/EXIF date only (not file modified/upload time). Photos with
            no date go to Undated/. Empty legacy folders can be removed when done.
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
          

          

            
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
          

          

          

          


          
Locked / inaccessible files

          

            Some NAS files (often under Google Drive Old) deny read access from Windows.
            Preview then remove them from the app index only — files stay on the drive
            until you unlock or delete them in the My Cloud dashboard.

File Converter

        

        

          
RAW / misnamed camera files → JPEG

          

            Converts camera RAW files and misnamed exports (e.g. JPEGs with a
            .NEF extension) into proper JPEGs in dated folders.
            Originals move to _quarantine_raw (recoverable) so Browse
            stays clean without keeping unused RAW.
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
          

          

            
            
            
          

          

          

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
Settings

        

        

          
My Cloud photo root

          

            Enable Local Network Access on your My Cloud Home, map the share in Windows,
            then paste the path to your photos folder (drive letter or UNC).
          

          

            
            
          

          
Scan performance

          

            How many photos to analyze at once over SMB. On My Cloud Home, 1–2 is often
            faster than 3+. Does not change scan detail — only concurrency. Takes effect on the next scan.
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
          

          


          
Fill missing dates

          

            Many photos have no camera EXIF date but live in folders like
            2026/2026-02, or have dates in the filename
            (e.g. screenshots). Preview then apply to set dates in the library index
            so year filters and “Date · newest” work. Does not rewrite the image files.
            Existing EXIF dates are never overwritten.
          

          

            
            
          

          

          


          
Junk & corrupt cleanup

          

            Permanently deletes macOS ._* sidecar junk, OS junk files, and
            unindexed media that cannot be opened (truncated/corrupt).
            Indexed photos in your library are not touched. Preview first — delete cannot be undone.
